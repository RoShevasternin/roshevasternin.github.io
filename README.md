# Lewydo™ — Love What You Do

The website of **Lewydo**, a two-person indie game studio from Ukraine: Vlad codes, Lilya designs.

**https://lewydo.com/** — our games in their own worlds (CubePix and Orbit Dash can be played right on the page),
the studio, our story and how to reach us, in every language of our games (82).

- 🎨 [CubePix](https://cubepix.lewydo.com/) — reveal famous paintings cube by cube ([web demo](https://cubepix.lewydo.com/play/))
- 🪐 [Orbit Dash](https://orbitdash.lewydo.com/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=page&utm_campaign=studio_readme) — two orbits, one tap ([Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.lewydo.orbitdash&referrer=utm_source%3Dgithub%26utm_medium%3Dpage%26utm_campaign%3Dstudio_readme))
- ▶️ [All our games on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/dev?id=5953840215091948966)
- 💚 [The Lewydo brand](https://lewydo.com/brand/) — how to recognise it, and the kit every Lewydo game uses:
  the pictures, the LibGDX atlas, the splash screen, the menu signature, About us and the heartbeat ([`brand/`](brand/), [brand.json](https://lewydo.com/brand/brand.json))
