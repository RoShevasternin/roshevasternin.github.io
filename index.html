// Lewydo™ — Love What You Do
package com.lewydo.brand

object LewydoHeart {
    const val GLOW = 0xFF6DF593
    const val SLOGAN = "Love What You Do"

    // one beat when the brand appears
    fun beat(heart: Actor, glow: Actor) {
        heart.addAction(sequence(
            scaleTo(1.13f, 1.13f, 0.10f, sineOut),
            scaleTo(1.00f, 1.00f, 0.12f, sineIn),
            scaleTo(1.07f, 1.07f, 0.10f, sineOut),
            scaleTo(1.00f, 1.00f, 0.12f, sineIn)))
        glow.addAction(sequence(
            delay(0.05f),
            scaleTo(1.15f, 1.15f, 0.12f, sineOut),
            scaleTo(1.05f, 1.05f, 0.28f, sineIn)))
    }

    fun makeGame(idea: Idea): Game {
        val game = code(idea) + design(idea)
        return game.withLove()
    }
}

Story

Our story so far

  1. 2017

    The first line of Java

    Vlad writes his first code. A year later he switches to Kotlin and stays with it for good.

  2. Spring 2021

    All in on game development

    Vlad leaves the Military Institute of Telecommunications behind and goes full-time into games: trainee, junior… all the way to Team Lead.

  3. 14 · 10 · 2022

    Vlad ❤ Lilya

    A developer meets a designer. The studio doesn't know it exists yet.

  4. 2025

    Lewydo is born

    Vlad always ended his Medium articles on LibGDX, Android and Kotlin the same way: «Love what you do» and a green heart 💚. That sign-off became our brand.

  5. 21 · 04 · 2026

    IMC is out

    IMC: Idle Merge Cubes 2026 is released on Google Play.

  6. 03 · 05 · 2026

    The idea of CubePix

    Vlad is serving in the Armed Forces of Ukraine. One morning he wakes up in a house where his unit is staying on an assignment — with the idea of CubePix in his head.

  7. 14 · 07 · 2026

    Married 💍

    Same day, different month — a team forever.

  8. 30 · 08 · 2026

    Orbit Dash takes off

    We make Orbit Dash and publish it on Google Play: two orbits, one tap.

  9. 30 · 09 · 2026

    CubePix comes to life

    The first playable prototype: famous paintings revealed cube by cube.

  10. 01 · 10 · 2026

    Pix is born

    We invent the CubePix mascot: a little pink painter cube with a beret and a brush. His name is Pix — Cube + Pix = CubePix, so the game carries his name.

  11. October 2026

    CubePix on Google Play

    Coming this month — the exact date very soon.
