How to recognise Lewydo

A hand-drawn green heart that speaks — it has a speech-bubble tail — glowing mint on deep night blue, with a round white name under it. Wherever you see it, the game was made by the two of us, with love.

  1. The heartDrawn by hand, shaped like a speech bubble: dark ink, a green fill with a chalky texture, a few splashes of paint.
  2. The glowA soft mint glow (#6DF593) around the heart — it is part of the mark, never a separate effect.
  3. The name«Lewydo» in Nunito Black, white, with a soft shadow.

Rules

Keep it the same

Do

  • Use the pictures and the code of the kit as they are.
  • Keep the colours, the font and the proportions: the Lockup is 208×322, the signature 24 + name.
  • Let the heart beat once when the brand appears: the splash, About us (every time it opens), the menu pill.
  • Theme the background and the ambient light to your game — the mark stays the same on top of it.
  • Keep it on a dark background (#0E1024 or your game's own dark night).

Don't

  • Redraw, recolour, stretch, squash, rotate or add effects to the mark.
  • Translate the slogan or set the name in another font.
  • Loop the heartbeat — a logo that blinks forever distracts from the game.
  • Put it on a light or busy background.

Heartbeat

One beat, when the brand appears

The heart and its glow beat separately — a quick «lub-dub» — exactly once: when the splash shows, every time About us opens, when the menu pill appears. Never in a loop. With «reduce motion» on, it doesn't beat at all.

For developers

Put Lewydo into a new game

Everything below lives in the site's repository → brand/ and in brand.json (all the numbers, texts in 15 languages and file addresses, for code and for Claude).

  1. The atlasCopy kit/atlas/brand.atlas + brand.png into assets/atlas/; its regions: brand_back, brand_front, lewydo, slogan, brand_line (your assetsBrand, EnumAtlas.BRAND).
  2. The codeCopy LewydoHeartbeat.kt (package com.lewydo.brand) and BrandScreen.kt, ABrandGroup.kt, ABrandLogo.kt; replace yourgame with your package. They use the Lewydo LibGDX base (AdvancedScreen, AConstraintLayout, AAutoLayout).
  3. The splash firstStart the game on BrandScreen: ≈ 3 s, one heartbeat, then it opens LoaderScreen.
  4. The menu signatureThe pill at the bottom of the menu (centred, 34 px from the bottom) → About us; one beat when the menu appears.
  5. About usThe Lockup at top 108, the studio text (English is the reference; all 15 languages are in brand.json), «Our website» → https://roshevasternin.github.io/ with the address under it, «Our games on Google Play» → our developer page, «© {{year}} Lewydo™». One beat every time it opens.
  6. Your game's worldTheme the background and the light behind the mark to your game — never the mark itself.
