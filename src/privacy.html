Privacy · lewydo.com

Updated 12 November 2026

The short version. This website (lewydo.com, the studio's page and its brand page) shows no ads, sets no cookies and runs no analytics. The only thing it sends anywhere is one small anonymous message to our own visit counter, which stores nothing that identifies you. Your browser keeps only your own choices for this site (see “On your device” below); they never leave it. Our games have their own policies: CubePix and Orbit Dash.

Visit counter

To know roughly how many people visit this site and where they come from, each page (the main page, the brand page, this page and the “page not found” page) sends one small anonymous message to our own counter at stats.lewydo.com, which Cloudflare runs for us (Cloudflare Workers). The same counter serves our games' websites.

  • What is counted: that a page was opened (which kind of page); the country, as the two-letter code that Cloudflare derives from your connection; and a coarse source label: the campaign tag in the link (utm_source) if the address has one; otherwise only the kind of site you came from, such as “google”, “tiktok”, one of our own sites or “other”; or — when the page is opened inside the TikTok, Instagram or Facebook app — that app's name, worked out on your device. The browser's name itself is never part of the message. Never the page address, the full referring address, your name or anything you type.
  • What is not used: no cookies and no storage on your device, no random or device ID, no fingerprinting. Cloudflare sees your IP address to deliver the message, as any server does; the counter does not store it, and its server keeps no logs. Your browser's user agent is checked on the server only to skip search-engine robots and is not stored.
  • Retention: each count is stored as one anonymous row (the fields above and the time), which we only ever read as totals, such as visits per day and per country; Cloudflare deletes these rows after about 3 months.
  • Legal basis: our legitimate interest in knowing, in aggregate, whether people find the site (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)); the data cannot be linked to you.

On your device

The site remembers the language you picked and that it has already shown the hint by the song's record, in your browser's localStorage (lewydo:lang, cubepix-site:lang — so CubePix's site opens in the same language — and lewydo:music). These stay in your browser, are not sent to us or anyone, and you can clear them with your browser's site data. The visit counter does not read or write any of them.

Things that load from other sites

The Orbit Dash block on the main page shows the real game from its own site, orbitdash.lewydo.com, which has its own policy. A song on the main page opens YouTube's own player (from youtube-nocookie.com) only when you tap it. The stream in our story (“Our first game review on a stream”) opens Twitch's own player (from player.twitch.tv, under Twitch's privacy notice) only when you tap “Watch the stream”; until then nothing loads from Twitch.
